Bible Versions Included

1. NKJV (New King James Version): An update to the King James Version that maintains its traditional language and style but with modernized wording to improve readability and understanding.

2. KJV (King James Version): The 1611 translation commissioned by King James I of England, known for its majestic prose and significant influence on English-speaking Christian communities.

3. ASV (American Standard Version): An early 20th-century revision of the KJV, known for its commitment to translating the Bible as accurately as possible, with a focus on textual fidelity.

4. WEB (World English Bible): A modern English translation that aims to be accessible and easy to read while maintaining a balance between accuracy and readability.
